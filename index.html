01

SwimThru (stereoscopic reef)

  • Stereoscopic LED wall
  • Unity 6 · URP
  • Xbox controller

A swimmable Hawaiian reef for the 6-meter stereoscopic LED wall in UH's Laboratory for Advanced Visualization & Applications (LAVA). Visitors swim through the reef, aim at sea life to read cards with Hawaiian, common, and scientific names, and play a recipe game, gathering ingredients from the reef for traditional creations like lei pūpū, luheʻe, and leiomano.

  • Comfort-first swimming for a shared audience: water-like inertia, no roll, auto-leveling pitch
  • Info cards and selection ring placed in world space at comfortable stereo depth
  • Procedural reef builder: spur-and-groove seafloor, flocking fish schools, animated caustics
  • Custom URP shaders for fish swimming, light shafts, marine snow, and the water surface

02

Renovation Walkthrough (VR)

  • Meta Quest
  • Room-scale VR
  • Design visualization

A true-scale VR model of a client's bathroom renovation, built from their drawings before construction. In the headset, the client could walk around their future space and flip between design options with the controller, to see how each choice would feel in the actual room.

  • Five switchable options combining tile schemes and vanities, swapped on the same meshes so the baked lighting stays valid
  • Real product textures for the tile, vanity, and pebble floor
  • Whole room generated by an editor tool from dimension constants, so design changes rebuild in one click
